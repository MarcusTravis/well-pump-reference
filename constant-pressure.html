Constant-pressure systems

Variable-speed systems that hold steady water pressure instead of cycling between a cut-in and cut-out. Rows are complete systems or drive-and-pump combinations, as each maker sells them.

Data last checked: Nov 22, 2026 · What changed

In short. A constant-pressure system uses a variable-frequency drive (VFD) to speed the pump up or down and hold one set pressure, instead of letting pressure swing between a switch’s cut-in and cut-out. It usually needs only a small pressure tank. This chart compares 30 systems, drives and all-in-one boosters from 5 brands. The drive must be matched to the motor it runs. The 27 rows with a live retailer price (checked Nov 22, 2026) ran from $944 to $9,206, with a median of $3,171. That is equipment only, not installation. See the FAQ and sizing basics for more.

Safety note. This is a reference, not installation advice. Confirm your well's depth, water level, recovery rate, casing size, and your electrical supply (voltage, phase, wire size, breaker) with a licensed well or pump installer and electrician before buying or installing anything.
