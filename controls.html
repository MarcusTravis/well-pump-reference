Controls: switches, control boxes, VFDs

The parts that start, stop, and protect the pump: pressure switches, 3-wire control boxes, protection devices, and drives sold on their own. Match the rating to your pump’s HP and voltage.

Data last checked: Nov 15, 2026 · What changed

In short. Controls start, stop and protect the pump. A pressure switch turns it on at the cut-in pressure and off at cut-out (30/50 and 40/60 psi are common factory settings). A 3-wire submersible motor needs a control box matched to its brand, horsepower and voltage. Pump protectors guard against dry running and bad voltage, and VFDs give constant pressure. This chart compares 53 controls from 9 brands. The 37 rows with a live retailer price (checked Nov 15, 2026) ran from $21 to $2,503, with a median of $296. That is equipment only, not installation. Safety note. This is a reference, not installation advice. Confirm your well's depth, water level, recovery rate, casing size, and your electrical supply (voltage, phase, wire size, breaker) with a licensed well or pump installer and electrician before buying or installing anything.
