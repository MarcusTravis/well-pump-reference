Pumps that sit down in the well and push water up. One row per commonly sold model, with flow, head, well size, warranty, and price, and links to each maker’s own curve or data sheet.

Safety note. This is a reference, not installation advice. Confirm your well's depth, water level, recovery rate, casing size, and your electrical supply (voltage, phase, wire size, breaker) with a licensed well or pump installer and electrician before buying or installing anything. Specs change — check the manufacturer's current page.

Price rule: Price = lowest in-stock price for a new unit of that exact model seen on a public US retailer or manufacturer-store listing (seller named and linked), checked on the date shown. No affiliate links. If no live price was found, a $ band is shown as an editorial estimate.
