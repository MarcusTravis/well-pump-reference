Submersible pumps

Pumps that sit down in the well and push water up. One row per commonly sold model, with flow, head, well size, warranty, and price, and links to each maker’s own curve or data sheet.

Data last checked: Nov 15, 2026 · What changed

In short. A submersible well pump sits under water inside the well casing and pushes water up, so it is not limited by suction the way an above-ground pump is. That makes it the usual choice once the water is deeper than about 25 ft. This chart compares 101 residential submersibles for 4-inch wells from 8 brands, 1/2 to 2 HP, including 2-wire models (no control box) and 3-wire models (need a matching control box above ground). Pick by flow at your total head, using the official curve each row links to. The 55 complete pump-and-motor units with a live retailer price (checked Nov 15, 2026) ran from $691 to $3,912, with a median of $1,300. That is equipment only, not installation. See the FAQ and sizing basics for more.

Safety note. This is a reference, not installation advice. Confirm your well's depth, water level, recovery rate, casing size, and your electrical supply (voltage, phase, wire size, breaker) with a licensed well or pump installer and electrician before buying or installing anything.
