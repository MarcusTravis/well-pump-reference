Pressure tanks

Diaphragm, bladder, and air-over-water tanks. Compare drawdown, not just total gallons — drawdown is the water you actually get between pump starts, and it depends on the pressure setting.

Data last checked: Nov 15, 2026 · What changed

In short. A pressure tank stores water under air pressure so the pump does not start every time a tap opens. Size it by drawdown, the water delivered between cut-in and cut-out, not by total gallons: aim for about one minute of pump run time per cycle, so a 10 gpm pump wants roughly 10 gallons of drawdown (about a 40-gallon-plus tank at 40/60 psi). This chart compares 66 tanks from 8 brands. The 26 rows with a live retailer price (checked Nov 15, 2026) ran from $151 to $2,639, with a median of $888. That is equipment only, not installation. See the FAQ and sizing basics for more.

Safety note. This is a reference, not installation advice. Confirm your well's depth, water level, recovery rate, casing size, and your electrical supply (voltage, phase, wire size, breaker) with a licensed well or pump installer and electrician before buying or installing anything.
