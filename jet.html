Jet pumps

Above-ground pumps that pull water up with suction, plus deep-well versions that use an ejector in the well. Check the Well filter: shallow-well jets only work when the water is within suction range.

Safety note. This is a reference, not installation advice. Confirm your well's depth, water level, recovery rate, casing size, and your electrical supply (voltage, phase, wire size, breaker) with a licensed well or pump installer and electrician before buying or installing anything.
