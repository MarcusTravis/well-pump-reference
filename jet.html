Jet pumps

Above-ground pumps that pull water up with suction, plus deep-well versions that use an ejector in the well. Check the Well filter: shallow-well jets only work when the water is within suction range.

Data last checked: Nov 15, 2026 · What changed

In short. A jet pump sits above ground and draws water up by suction. A shallow-well jet works to about 25 ft of lift; a convertible jet can serve a deeper well with an ejector placed down the well, at lower flow as depth grows. This chart compares 58 shallow-well and convertible jet pumps from 9 brands, all dual-voltage 115/230 V, with flow at the lift and pressure each maker states. The 45 rows with a live retailer price (checked Nov 15, 2026) ran from $358 to $2,226, with a median of $999. That is equipment only, not installation. See the FAQ and sizing basics for more.

Safety note. This is a reference, not installation advice. Confirm your well's depth, water level, recovery rate, casing size, and your electrical supply (voltage, phase, wire size, breaker) with a licensed well or pump installer and electrician before buying or installing anything.
